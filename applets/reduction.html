The Reduction Machine

Every recursive definition in Lean is a set of rewrite rules. Running a program means applying them until nothing matches. Watch it happen one step at a time — the highlighted piece is the redex, the subterm about to be replaced.

The payoff: two reverses

Exercises 3.1 and 3.10 both reverse a list. They are not the same program. Press Race and watch the step counts diverge — this is asymptotic complexity, made visible.

The naive version rebuilds the whole list on every step (that's the ++), so it does work proportional to n². The accumulator version carries the answer downward and touches each element once. Same output, different cost — and in Lean you can prove they agree (try it, after Chapter 8).
