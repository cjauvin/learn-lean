Reading the Goal State

A tactic proof looks like nonsense until you can see what it's doing to the goal. In VS Code the InfoView shows this live; here it's frozen so you can step back and forth. New hypotheses are green; a changed goal is amber.

Every state below was captured from Lean 4 itself with trace_state — this is exactly what your editor will show you.

Notice the pattern: every tactic either moves something from below the ⊢ to above it (intro, cases, induction), or rewrites what's below it (simp, rw, omega), or closes a goal outright (exact, rfl). That's the whole vocabulary. When you're stuck, ask which of those three you're trying to do.
